# Verified opening figures

Checked 15 November 2026. The accompanying JSON files preserve the exact Statistics Finland API responses.

1. **646,392 residents with a foreign mother tongue (2025).** Statistics Finland, Population structure, table 11ra: whole country / 2025 / Foreign-language speakers. The definition excludes Finnish, Swedish and Sámi. This is **not** a count of people unable to speak Finnish; registered mother tongue is not proficiency.
   https://pxdata.stat.fi/PXWeb/pxweb/en/StatFin/StatFin__vaerak/11ra.px
2. **Approximately 7% of adults**, annual prevalence of substantially disabling social anxiety, as stated in Duodecim's clinical reference updated 1 October 2025. This is a clinical prevalence estimate, **not a newly measured Finnish national headcount** and not specifically fear of seeking healthcare. The narration does not turn it into either.
   https://www.terveyskirjasto.fi/dlk00492
3. **247,980 residents of sparsely populated rural areas (2025).** Statistics Finland table 11s3: whole country / both sexes / all ages / category M7. Wider rural areas total 1,410,954, but that broader count is not used as a synonym for remote.
   https://pxdata.stat.fi/PXWeb/pxweb/en/StatFin/StatFin__vaerak/11s3.px
4. **3,000–6,000 undocumented people**, LSV's estimate. Not a census or an exact count.
   https://lsv.fi/en/healthcare-for-undocumented-migrants/
5. **A first step in local libraries/community centres** is the project's placement proposal. No invented national statistic about the absence of healthcare in libraries is stated. No suggestion that Edge-Care is already deployed in those places.

The groups overlap; their numbers must not be added together.
