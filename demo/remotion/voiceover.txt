Care starts with trust. An estimated three to six thousand undocumented people live in Finland. Language, information and trust can stand between a person and care.

Edge-Care is an anonymous companion. At a kiosk, or on your own phone, you can speak or type in your language. Read a letter on the device. The local flow continues when the network disappears.

To seal a referral, draw your signature and choose four digits. Confirm the code. There is no account and no recovery channel. Your device encrypts the template and creates a commitment. You are the key.

A clinician sees an anonymous summary and a recommended next step. The signature and code stay out of the doctor’s CRM.

At the appointment, draw again and enter your code. The device checks the template and commitment. The shared registry accepts a single spend. Offline verification stays provisional until synchronization.

On your own phone, the same flow adds an encrypted referral wallet. This is Edge-Care: a Junction demonstration of a more approachable first step. Guidance is not diagnosis, and access to care depends on individual circumstances.
